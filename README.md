# E-commerce-Sales-Performance-Analysis-2023-2025
This analysis examines 1,200 orders, highlighting sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, marketing channels, promotions, and cancellations. It provides insights and recommendations to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and revenue.
# Key Insights

**1. Customer purchases are mainly concentrated in lower-value transactions.**

Of the 1,200 orders recorded, **691 orders (57.6%)** fall within the **10–1,010** total price range. This shows that most customers tend to make relatively low-value purchases, while higher-value transactions occur less frequently.

**2. Sales performance reached its highest point in June.**

June recorded the **highest number of orders (147)** as well as the **highest revenue (170,616.13)**. This indicates particularly strong customer demand during the month and could reflect seasonal purchasing patterns.

**3. Revenue experienced a substantial decline in the second half of the year.**

Revenue decreased considerably from **170,616.13 in June** to **75,493.43 in November**. This decline suggests that customer purchasing activity weakened after the middle of the year, highlighting an opportunity to introduce targeted promotions during slower periods.

**4. Chairs are the highest-performing product category.**

Chairs generated the **highest revenue (195,620.11)** and recorded the **largest quantity sold (562 units)**. This demonstrates strong customer demand and positions Chairs as the most valuable product category in terms of overall sales performance.

**5. Laptops have the highest average spending per transaction.**

Laptops recorded the **highest Average Order Value (1,110.56)** across all product categories. This suggests that customers who purchase laptops typically spend more per transaction than customers purchasing other products.

**6. Instagram is the most effective referral channel.**

Instagram generated the **highest revenue (275,285.45)** among the referral sources analyzed. This indicates that Instagram is currently the strongest channel for attracting customers who generate revenue.

**7. Free shipping is the most successful promotional offer.**

The **FREESHIP** coupon generated the highest revenue at **335,036.99**. This suggests that customers respond positively to free-shipping incentives and may find them more attractive than conventional discount offers.

**8. High-value transactions influence the overall average order value.**

The **mean order value of 1,053.97** is considerably higher than the **median of 823.62**. Although only **8 orders (0.67%)** were classified as outliers, these unusually high-value purchases contribute to raising the overall average. This reflects a **positively skewed distribution**, where a small number of high-value transactions have a noticeable effect on overall spending.

# Recommendations

**1. Strengthen Marketing During Low-Sales Periods**

Since revenue reached its peak in June and declined significantly during the latter part of the year, the company should introduce targeted marketing campaigns, seasonal promotions, and customer-retention strategies from July to November. These initiatives could help stimulate demand and maintain more consistent sales throughout the year.

**2. Focus on High-Performing Products**

Chairs generated the highest overall revenue, while laptops recorded the highest average order value. The company should prioritize stock availability, promotional activities, and product-bundling strategies for these categories to maximize sales and profitability.

**3. Invest More in Instagram Marketing**

Instagram generated the highest revenue among the referral channels. The company should consider increasing its investment in Instagram advertising, influencer collaborations, and targeted promotional campaigns to attract more customers and generate additional revenue.

**4. Expand Free Shipping Promotions**

The FREESHIP promotion generated the highest revenue among the available coupon campaigns, indicating strong customer interest in shipping incentives. The company could expand free-shipping offers, particularly by applying them to orders above a specific spending threshold to encourage customers to increase their purchase value.

**5. Reduce Order Cancellations**

With a cancellation rate of **20.8%**, a considerable amount of potential revenue may be lost through cancelled orders. The company should identify the major reasons for cancellations and improve inventory management, order processing, and customer communication to minimize cancellations.

**6. Improve Order Fulfillment and Customer Experience**

The number of cancelled and returned orders being higher than successfully delivered orders may indicate weaknesses in the fulfillment process. Improving product quality control, delivery reliability, order accuracy, and product descriptions could help reduce returns and cancellations while improving customer satisfaction.

**7. Encourage Higher-Value Purchases**

Since a large proportion of transactions are relatively low in value, the company should use cross-selling, upselling, and product-bundling strategies to encourage customers to spend more per order. Spending thresholds and incentives for larger purchases could also help increase the average order value.

**8. Maintain Diverse Payment Options**

The relatively balanced use of different payment methods highlights the importance of providing customers with flexibility. The company should continue offering a variety of secure payment options and consider installment plans for higher-priced products to make larger purchases more accessible and improve conversion rates.
